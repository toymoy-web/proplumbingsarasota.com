The Symptom Bridge

If This Sounds Familiar, It Might Be a Slab Leak

🌡

Warm Spot on the Floor

A warm patch on tile or concrete flooring, especially near where a hot water line runs, is one of the clearest slab leak signs.

💧

Water Sound With Everything Off

Hearing running water when every fixture in the house is off points to a leak somewhere in the supply lines, and a slab leak is one of the more common causes.

📊

Unexplained Bill Increase

A meaningful jump in the water bill with no visible leak anywhere inside the house is a common way a slab leak first gets noticed.

Local Cause

Why This Happens in Sarasota

Sarasota's sandy soil and high water table let the ground under a slab shift slightly over time, and that movement stresses the water lines running through or under the concrete. Older copper lines already weakened by the area's salt-air corrosion are especially vulnerable to developing a pinhole leak once that ground movement puts pressure on a joint.

Locating It Without Demolition

Detection Before Any Concrete Gets Cut

We use acoustic listening equipment and a meter isolation test to narrow a slab leak's location to a specific section before recommending where to open the concrete. This matters because the wrong guess means cutting concrete in the wrong spot, then cutting it again in the right one.

Repair Options

Spot Repair vs. Reroute

🔧

Spot Repair

For a single, accessible leak point, opening a small section of the slab to repair that exact spot is usually the least invasive option.

♻

Reroute Above the Slab

For an older line or one showing multiple weak points, rerouting the water line above the slab through walls or the attic avoids repeat slab openings down the road.

Don't Wait On This

Why a Slab Leak Gets Worse, Not Better

A slab leak doesn't stay small. Left alone, it continues to erode the surrounding soil and can eventually undermine the slab itself, while the constant moisture creates the same mold risk as any other standing-water event in Sarasota's humidity. Confirming and repairing it early is meaningfully cheaper than waiting until flooring or drywall has to be replaced along with the pipe.

After the Repair

Restoring the Floor

Once the leak itself is repaired, restoring the opened section of slab and matching flooring is the final step, and we coordinate that so the visible result matches the surrounding floor as closely as possible rather than leaving a patched-looking section behind.

Pricing

Every job is priced after detection. The real factors: which detection method the leak needed, whether a spot repair or a full reroute above the slab is the right fix, how accessible the leak location is, and flooring restoration afterward.

Common Questions

Slab Leak Detection & Repair FAQs

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

A warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water with everything off, or an unexplained jump in the water bill are the three most common signs. Any of these is worth a detection visit.

Do you have to break up the whole floor to fix a slab leak?

No. Acoustic detection and meter isolation narrow the leak to a specific section first, so any concrete work is limited to that spot rather than an exploratory tear-up.

Is a spot repair or rerouting the pipe the better option?

It depends on the age and condition of the line. A single leak on an otherwise sound line usually gets a spot repair; an older line with signs of multiple weak points is often better rerouted above the slab entirely.

How much does slab leak repair cost in Sarasota?

It depends on the repair method (spot repair vs. reroute) and how accessible the leak location is. We give you a price after detection, before any concrete is opened.
