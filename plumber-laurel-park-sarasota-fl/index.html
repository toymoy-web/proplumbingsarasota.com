The Neighborhood

A Historic District Close to Downtown

The Laurel Park Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places on March 11, 2008. Streets named as its boundaries include Morrill Street, Orange Avenue, Brother Geenen Way, Julia Place and Lafayette Court. The City of Sarasota also created a Laurel Park Overlay District, which the neighborhood association describes as a way to protect the area's quiet residential character and historical significance.

The housing is a real mix. Descriptions of Laurel Park range from workman cottages to Mediterranean Revival to modern, with single-family houses, guest houses, duplexes and apartment buildings side by side. Much of the historic building stock dates to the 1920s, and Towles Court, the artists' colony with galleries and a monthly gallery walk, is part of the neighborhood's identity.

What It Means for Plumbing

An Older, Mixed Neighborhood Needs a Look, Not a Guess

We don't assume a house is in poor shape because it is old. Many houses from the 1920s have been renovated more than once, so the pipes behind one wall can be decades newer than the pipes behind the next. The reliable way to find out is to look: a pressure check at the main, a visual check of any exposed supply lines, and a camera run through the drain line when there is a recurring problem.

Duplexes, guest houses and apartment buildings add a second question, which is whose pipe it is. A leak that shows up in one unit can start in a shared line, so on a multi-unit property we trace the problem to its source first. That way the right party pays for the right repair.

If your project touches the exterior of a house or involves new construction, questions about historic review and permits belong with the City of Sarasota and the Laurel Park neighborhood association. We can describe the plumbing side of the work so you can have that conversation with accurate details.

Common Questions

Laurel Park Plumbing FAQs

Is Laurel Park a historic district?

Yes. The Laurel Park Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places on March 11, 2008, and the City of Sarasota has a separate Laurel Park Overlay District. Whether either one affects a particular project is a question for the City.

Do you work on duplexes and small apartment buildings in Laurel Park?

Yes. Laurel Park includes duplexes and apartment buildings alongside single-family houses. We trace a leak or drain problem to its source first, since in a multi-unit building it can start in a shared line.

Do older houses in Laurel Park need repiping?

Not automatically. Age alone doesn't say what is inside the walls, because many older houses have had partial updates. We recommend repiping only when what we find supports it.
