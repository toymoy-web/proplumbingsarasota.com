What We Fix

Common Faucet & Sink Calls

💧

Dripping or Leaking Faucet

Almost always a worn cartridge, O-ring, or valve seat, accelerated by hard-water mineral buildup inside the fixture.

⚠

Shut-Off Valve Won't Turn

A supply valve under a sink that's seized or won't fully shut off is a real problem the next time a repair or emergency requires isolating that fixture.

🚿

Low Water Pressure at One Fixture

When pressure is low at a single sink but normal elsewhere, the cause is usually a clogged aerator or a partially closed valve, not a house-wide pressure problem.

Local Cause

Why Hard Water Wears Fixtures Faster

Sarasota's water hardness leaves mineral deposits inside faucet cartridges and shut-off valves. Why fixtures here often need cartridge replacement sooner than the manufacturer's typical lifespan would suggest. A faucet that drips despite being handled gently is usually mineral wear on the internal seal, not a defect in the fixture itself.

Repair or Replace

When a New Fixture Makes More Sense

Repairable Faucets

Most name-brand faucets have replaceable internal cartridges, so a repair is usually the faster and cheaper fix for a drip or a stiff handle.

When Replacement Is Cleaner

Older or discontinued fixtures without available replacement parts, or a faucet with visible corrosion at the base, are usually more cost-effective to replace outright than to keep repairing.

Beyond the Faucet

Related Fixture Repairs We Handle

🛁

Sink Basin Leaks

A leak where the sink basin meets the countertop or a drain assembly is a separate seal repair from a faucet issue, and both are often checked together during the same visit.

🔩

Supply Line Replacement

The flexible braided supply lines connecting a fixture to the shut-off valve have a service life too, and replacing an aging one during a faucet repair prevents a future failure at the connection.

🛀

Bathtub and Shower Valves

A shower valve that's hard to turn, drips after use, or won't hold a consistent temperature involves the same cartridge-wear pattern as a kitchen or bathroom faucet.

What to Expect

A Straightforward Visit

Most faucet and fixture calls are a single-visit job: we identify the cause, confirm whether a cartridge, valve, or full fixture replacement is needed, and complete the work the same visit in most cases. For a fixture replacement, we'll also check the supply lines and shut-off valve at the same time. Replacing a worn faucet while leaving an aging supply line in place just moves the next failure a few months down the road instead of preventing it.

Pricing

What Affects the Price of Faucet & Sink Repair

Every job is priced after we've actually seen it. The real factors: whether a replacement cartridge is available for that specific fixture, whether the fixture itself needs full replacement, the condition of the supply lines, and how accessible the connection is.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden, accidental damage, like a burst pipe. They usually don't cover the underlying repair itself, or normal wear-related failures. It's worth checking your specific policy rather than assuming either way.

Common Questions

Faucet & Sink Repair FAQs

Why does my faucet keep dripping after I've had it repaired before?

If the drip returns within a year, hard-water mineral buildup on the new cartridge is the most likely cause. We can talk through whether a water softener would extend the life of future repairs.

Can you replace a faucet with one I've already purchased?

Yes, we install customer-supplied fixtures as long as they're a standard configuration for the existing plumbing.

My shut-off valve under the sink won't turn. Is that urgent?

Not urgent on its own, but it means you can't isolate that fixture in an emergency. We recommend replacing a seized valve before it's needed in a hurry, since a working shut-off is what lets you contain a leak yourself while waiting on us.

Do you repair commercial or property-management sink fixtures?

Yes, we handle both single-family and property-management calls, including work orders coordinated with a management company, and we can bill or schedule around a tenant's availability when needed.
