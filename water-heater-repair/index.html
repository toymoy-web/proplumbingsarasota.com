Common Faults

What We're Usually Called About

💧

Leaking at a Fitting

A leak at a pipe connection or the drain valve is often a simple fitting repair, not a sign the tank itself has failed, and it's worth checking before assuming replacement is needed.

🌡

No Hot Water

A failed heating element, thermostat, or, on a gas unit, a pilot light or igniter problem are the most common causes of water that won't heat at all.

🔊

Popping or Rumbling

Sediment hardened at the bottom of the tank from Sarasota's hard water causes a popping sound during heating and reduces efficiency, though a flush can sometimes resolve it if caught early.

How We Decide

Repairable vs. Not

Leak at the Tank Itself

A leak coming from a fitting, valve, or connection is repairable. A leak coming from the tank body itself, meaning the steel has actually corroded through, means the tank is done, no fitting repair fixes that, and we'll tell you so rather than attempting one.

Tankless-Specific Repairs

Tankless units in Sarasota's hard water most often need a descaling flush of the heat exchanger rather than a parts replacement. If a tankless unit is throwing an error code, we diagnose the code against the actual fault before assuming a part needs to be swapped.

Sarasota Factor

Why Repairs Happen Sooner Here

Hard water combined with near-constant heat means the anode rod that protects a tank from corrosion gets consumed faster here than in cooler, softer-water regions, and a spent anode rod is one of the most common precursors to a tank leak. Checking and replacing the anode rod proactively, before it's fully gone, is one of the cheaper repairs that extends a tank's life meaningfully.

Gas vs. Electric

Repair Differences by Unit Type

🔥

Gas Units

A pilot light that won't stay lit, a faulty thermocouple, or an igniter failure are the most common gas-unit repairs, and most are straightforward once diagnosed correctly.

⚡

Electric Units

Electric tanks more often fail at a heating element or thermostat, both replaceable parts that don't require draining and reinstalling the whole tank.

⚙

Safety Valve Checks

Whatever the unit type, we test the temperature and pressure relief valve during any repair visit. A stuck or failed safety valve is a real hazard independent of whatever brought us out originally.

What a Repair Visit Looks Like

From Diagnosis to Fix

Common Questions

Water Heater Repair FAQs

Is it worth repairing my water heater or should I replace it?

It depends on where the fault is. A fitting, thermostat, or heating element repair on a unit under about 8 years old is usually worth it. A leak from the tank body itself on an older unit means replacement is the only real option.

How much does a water heater repair typically cost?

It depends on the specific part or fitting involved. We diagnose the actual fault first and give you a firm price before doing any repair work.

Can a tankless water heater be repaired instead of replaced?

Yes, most tankless issues are a heat exchanger needing a descaling flush or a specific component failure, both of which are usually repairable rather than requiring full replacement.

Why does my water heater make a popping sound?

That's sediment that's hardened at the bottom of the tank, common in Sarasota's hard water. A flush can help if caught early; left too long, it accelerates tank wear.
