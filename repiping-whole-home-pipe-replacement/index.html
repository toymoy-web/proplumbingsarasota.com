The Symptom Bridge

Signs Your Pipes Are Past Patching

💧

Repeated Leaks in Different Spots

A leak fixed last year and a new one this year, in unrelated parts of the system, usually means the pipe material itself is failing, not bad luck.

🌋

Discolored or Rusty Water

Brown or rust-tinted water, especially right after the house has sat unused, points to corrosion inside galvanized supply pipes.

🚿

Low Pressure Throughout the House

Pressure that's dropped gradually across every fixture, not just one, often means internal corrosion has narrowed the pipe diameter over years.

Local Cause

Why Older Sarasota Homes Need This

Homes built before CPVC and PEX became standard often still run on galvanized steel or aging copper, and Sarasota's salt air and humidity accelerate the corrosion that eventually causes these repeated leaks and pressure loss. Once a galvanized system starts failing in one place, the rest of the system is usually not far behind. The whole run has been corroding at roughly the same rate.

Materials

What We Repipe With, and Why

⚙

CPVC

Resistant to the corrosion that affects metal pipe in salt air, and a common, cost-effective standard for Sarasota repipes.

🧱

PEX

Flexible, resistant to corrosion, and easier to route through existing walls with fewer joints than rigid pipe, often reducing labor time on a whole-home job.

🛤

HDPE for Main Lines

Preferred for the buried main line specifically, given how well it handles Sarasota's sandy, shifting soil compared to rigid materials.

What the Job Involves

Repair vs. Full Repipe

When a Repair Still Makes Sense

A single isolated leak on an otherwise sound system, especially a newer one, is still just a repair. Repiping is about the pattern, not any one leak on its own.

Permits and Process

A whole-home repipe requires a permit through the Sarasota Building Department under the Florida Plumbing Code, and we handle that filing. Work is typically done in sections to minimize how long any part of the home is without water, and walls are patched to drywall-ready condition once the new lines are in and pressure-tested.

Pricing

What Affects the Price of Repiping

Every job is priced after we've assessed the home. The real factors: the home's size and number of fixtures, material choice between CPVC and PEX, whether the buried main line also needs HDPE replacement, and how much wall access is required.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden, accidental damage, like a burst pipe. They usually don't cover the underlying repair itself, or normal wear-related failures. It's worth checking your specific policy rather than assuming either way.

Common Questions

Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe Replacement FAQs

How do I know if I need a full repipe instead of another repair?

Repeated leaks in different, unrelated parts of the home, discolored water, and gradually dropping pressure throughout the house are the strongest signs. One isolated leak on an otherwise sound system is usually still just a repair.

How long does a whole-home repipe take?

It depends on the home's size and layout, but most jobs are completed in sections over a few days so the household isn't without water the entire time.

Is PEX or CPVC better for a Sarasota home?

Both hold up well against local salt-air corrosion. PEX's flexibility often means fewer joints and faster installation; CPVC is a proven, cost-effective standard. We'll recommend based on your home's specific layout.

Will repiping require cutting into walls?

Yes, some wall access is necessary to run new lines. We patch to drywall-ready condition once the plumbing work and pressure testing are complete.
