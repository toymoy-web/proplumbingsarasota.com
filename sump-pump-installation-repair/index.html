The Symptom Bridge

Signs Your Sump Pump Needs Attention

🔇

It Doesn't Turn On During Rain

A pump that stays silent during heavy rain when it should be cycling is either not receiving power, has a failed float switch, or has already failed outright.

🔁

It Runs Constantly

A pump that never stops cycling, even without active rain, often means a stuck float switch or a pit that's taking on groundwater faster than expected.

💧

Water Still Gets In Despite It

If water intrusion continues even with the pump running, the pump may be undersized for the actual water volume, or the discharge line may be blocked or improperly routed.

Local Cause

Why This Is a Real Sarasota Concern

Sarasota's flat Gulf Coast terrain and naturally high water table mean groundwater sits closer to the surface here than in most cities, and heavy seasonal rain or hurricane-season storms push it higher still. For crawl spaces, low-lying lots, and properties near drainage-limited areas, a functioning sump pump is often the difference between a dry space and standing water after a serious storm.

Installation

Sizing and Backup Power

Correct Pump Sizing

A pump sized for the property's actual water intrusion rate, not a generic default, is what prevents the "running constantly but still flooding" problem. We assess the pit and expected water volume before recommending a specific pump.

Battery Backup

Hurricane-season power outages are exactly when a sump pump is needed most, and exactly when standard power can fail. A battery backup system keeps the pump running through an outage, which is worth discussing for any Sarasota property relying on one.

Repair

Common Sump Pump Failures

⚙

Failed Float Switch

The float switch that tells the pump when to turn on is one of the most common failure points, and it's usually a straightforward part replacement rather than a full pump swap.

🔌

Clogged Impeller

Debris in the pit can jam the pump's impeller, causing it to run without actually moving water. Clearing it is often enough to restore function.

⚠

Discharge Line Issues

A frozen, blocked, or improperly routed discharge line can make a functioning pump look like it's failed. The water has nowhere to actually go.

Common Questions

Sump Pump Installation & Repair FAQs

Does my Sarasota property actually need a sump pump?

It depends on your lot's elevation, drainage, and whether you have a crawl space or low-lying area prone to water intrusion. We can assess your specific property and tell you honestly if one is warranted.

Why does my sump pump run constantly?

Usually a stuck float switch or a pit taking on more groundwater than expected. It's worth having checked, since a pump running nonstop wears out faster and may mean it's not actually being effective.

What happens to my sump pump during a power outage?

Without a battery backup, it stops working entirely, which is a real risk since power outages often coincide with the heavy storms that make the pump necessary in the first place. A battery backup keeps it running through an outage.

How often should a sump pump be tested?

A quick test (pouring water into the pit to confirm the float switch triggers the pump) is worth doing before hurricane season each year, and immediately after any period of non-use.
