Mitigation vs. Restoration

Two Phases, Not One Job

Mitigation (This Page)

Mitigation is the emergency response: extracting standing water, setting up drying equipment, and stopping the damage from spreading further. It happens first, usually within hours of the call.

Restoration (What Follows)

Once the space is dry and stable, restoration addresses what's left, replacing materials that couldn't be saved and returning the space to its condition before the damage. Mitigation and restoration are often the same visit for a smaller event, or sequential phases for a larger one.

Local Cause

Why Speed Matters in Sarasota Specifically

Sarasota's flat terrain and high water table mean flooding from heavy rain or hurricane-season storms can happen fast and cover a wider area than in cities with better natural drainage. Combined with the area's constant humidity, standing water left even overnight gives mold a real head start. Why mitigation is treated as a true emergency rather than a next-day appointment.

What to Do While Waiting

Before Crews Arrive

🛑

Shut Off the Source If Safe

If the water is coming from inside the home and the main shutoff is reachable without walking through standing water, turn it off first.

⚡

Stay Clear of Electrical

Avoid standing water near outlets or a breaker panel, and shut off power to the affected area at the breaker only if it can be reached from dry ground.

📸

Photograph Before Moving Items

Quick photos of the affected area before moving furniture help with insurance and don't slow down our response time at all.

What Mitigation Involves

The First Hours

Water Extraction

Pumps and extraction equipment remove standing water as the very first step, before anything else begins.

Common Questions

Emergency Water Mitigation FAQs

What's the difference between water mitigation and water damage restoration?

Mitigation is the immediate emergency response: extracting water and starting the drying process to stop damage from spreading. Restoration is what follows once the space is stable, replacing what couldn't be saved and returning it to its prior condition.

How fast do you respond for mitigation in Sarasota?

We dispatch 24/7 for active flooding. Fast extraction in the first few hours is the single biggest factor in whether a space avoids a full mold problem afterward.

Is mitigation covered by homeowners insurance?

It depends on your specific policy and the cause of the water event. Sudden, accidental water damage is more commonly covered than damage from a long-term, unaddressed issue, so it's worth checking your policy directly.

Do I need a separate call for the plumbing repair if a pipe caused the flooding?

Yes. Fixing the pipe, valve, or fixture that caused the flooding is a standard plumbing repair at our regular line. Mitigation and restoration, reached at (833) 521-1033, only cover the cleanup and drying.
