The Symptom Bridge

Signs Sarasota Water Hardness Is the Real Cause

💧

White Spots and Film

Chalky white spots on faucets, glassware, and shower doors are classic mineral deposits from hard water, not a cleaning problem.

🧼

Dry Skin and Stiff Laundry

Hard water interferes with soap and detergent. Why skin can feel dry after showering and towels or clothes can feel stiff even when clean.

♻

Fixtures and Appliances Wearing Out Early

Faucets, water heaters, and dishwashers all fail sooner under sustained hard-water mineral exposure than the same models would elsewhere.

Options

Softener vs. Filtration: What's the Difference

Water Softener

A softener removes the calcium and magnesium minerals that cause hardness, using a resin bed that regenerates with salt on a schedule. This is the direct fix for scale buildup and soap-interference symptoms.

Whole-Home Filtration

A filtration system targets taste, odor, chlorine, and sediment, but doesn't remove hardness minerals on its own. Many Sarasota homes run both together: a softener for hardness, a filter for taste and sediment.

Repair

Common Softener Problems

Softener Not Regenerating

If water is suddenly hard again after months of soft water, the unit likely isn't regenerating on schedule. This is often a control valve or timer issue rather than a sign the whole system needs replacing.

Salt Bridging

A hardened crust of salt in the brine tank, common in humid climates like Sarasota, can prevent the salt from dissolving into the regeneration cycle even when the tank looks full. Clearing this is a quick fix once identified.

Installation

Sizing a System to Your Household

A softener sized too small for a household's actual water use regenerates constantly and burns through salt faster than expected, while an oversized unit costs more upfront than it needs to. We size based on household size, local water hardness readings, and actual usage patterns rather than a flat default recommendation, and we test your specific water hardness before recommending a system rather than assuming a citywide average applies to every address.

Maintenance

Keeping a Softener Running Well

Checking salt levels monthly and keeping the brine tank free of bridging are the two habits that prevent most softener service calls before they start. We walk new customers through both during installation, and a periodic check during any other visit to the home is a quick way to catch a developing problem before the water goes hard again.

Common Questions

Water Softener & Filtration Installation FAQs

How hard is the water in Sarasota?

Sarasota's municipal water is considered moderately to significantly hard, hard enough to cause visible scale on fixtures and accelerate wear on water heaters over time. A softener is a common, worthwhile addition here.

Do I need a softener and a filter, or just one?

It depends on your goals. A softener addresses hardness (scale, soap interference); a filter addresses taste, odor, and sediment. Many homes benefit from both, but a softener alone solves most of the symptoms people call about.

Why did my softener stop working suddenly?

The most common cause is the unit not regenerating on schedule, often a control valve or timer issue, or a hardened salt bridge in the brine tank preventing proper regeneration. Both are usually straightforward repairs.

Will a softener extend the life of my water heater?

Yes. Removing hardness minerals before they reach the water heater or tankless unit significantly reduces the scale buildup that shortens both types of units' service life.
