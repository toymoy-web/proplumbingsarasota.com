Common Reasons to Call

When a Camera Inspection Makes Sense

🏡

Before Buying a Home

An older Sarasota home's sewer lateral is one of the more expensive things to discover as a problem after closing. A pre-purchase inspection shows real condition before you own it.

🌳

Known Root-Intrusion Risk

Homes in mature, tree-lined neighborhoods with an older clay or cast-iron lateral benefit from a proactive check even without symptoms yet.

🔁

After a Repair, Before Trusting It's Done

Confirming a spot repair or trenchless liner actually resolved the issue, rather than assuming it did, is a common follow-up inspection request.

What You Get

A Real Look, Not Just a Verdict

The camera records the full length of the accessible line, and we walk through the footage with you rather than just handing over a pass or fail. If we see root intrusion, a bellied section, or a crack, we'll show you exactly where it is on the recording and explain what it means in practical terms, not just flag a problem and leave you to guess at the scope.

Local Cause

Why This Matters More in Established Sarasota Neighborhoods

Homes in neighborhoods like The Meadows and Whitfield, with mature tree cover and older clay or cast-iron laterals, are exactly where a camera inspection tends to find something worth knowing about even before a backup happens. Sarasota's sandy soil and high water table also make bellied, sagging sections more common here than in areas with firmer, more stable ground.

After the Inspection

What Happens With the Findings

A clean inspection means no immediate action needed, and you have a documented baseline for the future. If something is found, we explain the realistic repair options and their honest urgency, a small amount of early root growth is very different from an active belly or crack, and we'll never push a repair the footage doesn't actually support.

Equipment

How the Inspection Actually Works

A flexible camera on a push rod is fed through an existing cleanout or access point, transmitting live video as it travels the length of the line. The camera head includes its own lighting, since most sewer laterals have no ambient light at all, and a locator signal lets us mark the exact above-ground position of anything significant found. Matters if a repair ends up being needed later.

Pricing

What Affects the Price of Sewer Camera Inspection

Every job is priced after we understand the scope. The real factors: the line's length, how many access points are available, whether it's a standalone inspection or paired with another service, and whether a recorded copy of the footage is requested.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden, accidental damage, like a burst pipe. They usually don't cover the underlying repair itself, or normal wear-related failures. It's worth checking your specific policy rather than assuming either way.

Common Questions

Sewer Camera Inspection FAQs

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

Most residential inspections take under an hour, depending on the line's length and how many access points are available.

Do I get to see the footage, or just a summary?

You get to see the actual footage. We walk through it with you and point out anything worth noting, rather than just delivering a verdict.

Should I get an inspection before buying an older Sarasota home?

Yes, especially for a home with an unknown pipe age or history. A sewer lateral failure is one of the more expensive surprises a new homeowner can inherit, and an inspection before closing shows real condition.

What if the inspection finds a problem?

We explain what was found, how urgent it actually is, and the realistic repair options, from monitoring a small amount of root growth to scheduling a spot repair, without pushing an unnecessary fix.
